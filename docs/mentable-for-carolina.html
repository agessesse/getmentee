Mentable Talent is everywhere. Access isn’t.

Carolina already has the people.
What’s missing is the infrastructure between them.

Thousands of Carolina alumni would take a call from a student. Thousands of students would benefit from one. The difficult part has never been goodwill. It is finding the right person, giving the conversation a reason to happen, and keeping the relationship going after it.

What Mentable does

01
Activate
Bring alumni and students into one place by invitation.
02
Match
Pair on what a student needs and where an alum can genuinely help.
03
Structure
Goals, conversations and next steps live with the relationship.
04
Accountability
Each conversation ends with something specific to do.
05
Measure
Program leads see participation and momentum, never private messages.

How a Carolina deployment could work

  • Alumni volunteer and say what they can actually help someone navigate.
  • Students apply or are invited into a named program.
  • Matches are made deliberately, by a person, at this size.
  • Every relationship gets goals, sessions and agreed next steps.
  • Program leads see whether it is working without reading conversations.

One layer, several communities

A single installation can run more than one program at once, each with its own participants and its own lead:

  • Alumni and current students
  • Family business
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Finance and competitive recruiting
  • First-generation students
  • Veterans

What Carolina would learn

  • Who actually participates, on both sides
  • Whether matches turn into conversations
  • Whether students follow through between them
  • Where relationships stall, early enough to intervene
  • What participants say they got out of it
  • Whether students come back later as mentors

A possible pilot

Start deliberately small: one program, one semester, a number of pairs small enough that every match is made by hand and every relationship is watched closely. The point of a first program is not scale. It is finding out what actually happens, with enough structure to know.
